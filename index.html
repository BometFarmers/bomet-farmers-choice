Kalenjin Cultural Heritage • Rift Valley

The Sacred Art of Sotet & Charcoal-Cured Mursik

In Kalenjin tradition, Mursik is far more than fermented milk—it is a symbol of hospitality, celebration, and athletic endurance. Crafted exclusively from rich evening milk of pasture-fed highland cows, our Mursik follows centuries-old ancestral techniques passed down by Kalenjin dairy artisans.

1

Gourd Curing (Sotet)

Hollowed calabash gourds are sun-dried and scrubbed clean using fibrous palm twigs (Sosiot) to prepare the natural vessel.

2

Senetwet Ash Infusion

Glowing embers from the indigenous Senetwet (Senna didymobotrya) tree are crushed inside the gourd, imparting natural antimicrobial smoke and deep charcoal flavor.

3

Slow Wild Fermentation

Boiled, cooled whole milk is sealed inside the smoked Sotet for 48–72 hours, yielding a velvety, probiotic-dense cultured delicacy.

Tasting Notes & Serving

Expect a thick, spoon-coating texture with a balanced tangy acidity, roasted wood-smoke aroma, and subtle earthy mineral notes from fine Senetwet ash specks. Best enjoyed chilled alongside warm brown ugali and roasted indigenous greens.
